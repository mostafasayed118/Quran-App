## Full description (≤4000 chars)

Quran Yahdi — your complete offline-first Islamic companion.

A full Mushaf, recitations, adhkar, duas, and prayer times — all in one light, simple app that never leaves you stranded without a connection.

📖 The Holy Quran
• Complete Uthmani text (114 surahs, 6,236 ayahs) bundled inside the app — works fully offline
• Fast surah index with instant search by name or number
• Verse-by-verse reading or the traditional paginated Mushaf mode
• Bookmarks and automatic last-read tracking
• Tafsir and translations right from the ayah sheet

🎧 Recitations
• 240+ reciters — stream instantly or download for offline listening
• A persistent mini player that follows you while you read
• A download manager with per-file status and delete
• Start playback from the ayah you were reading

🕌 Prayer times & Qibla
• Accurate times for any city you choose in one step
• Optional "nearest city" from your location — computed on-device, never sent anywhere
• Prayer reminders you can enable or disable anytime
• A live Qibla compass driven by your phone's sensors

📿 Adhkar & worship
• Morning, evening, and sleep adhkar with a per-item counter
• A digital tasbih with a clear circular dial
• Prophetic duas and ruqyah, organized and easy to read
• A worship tracker: the five daily prayers and your khatma progress

🌙 Thoughtful extras
• Comfortable dark mode, Arabic-first on every screen
• Zakat calculator with gold and silver nisab modes
• Hijri calendar with the Gregorian date
• The 99 names of Allah with their meanings
• Authentic mushaf typography and comfortable Arabic fonts at any size

Privacy matters: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no data collection — everything stays on your device.
