What this application stores
----------------------------

The stored copy departs from the source in one place, at a surah boundary:

  The source repeats the basmala at the head of the first verse of every
  surah. Here that first verse is stored without it, because the reader draws
  the basmala itself, once, as a surah header. Al-Fatiha is unaffected: there
  the basmala is verse 1 and is stored as verse 1.

Everything else is as received, including the sajda marks (۩).

Because of that omission the stored files are not a verbatim copy and must not
be passed on as one. Take the text from the Tanzil Project, not from this
application.
